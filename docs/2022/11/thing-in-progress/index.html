Thing in Progress

29 November 2022

https://poems.culturing.net/2022/11/thing-in-progress/

I shall pull back the curtain,
peep under the veil, at the thing
in its progress. It is not done yet,
and yet is the progression in it
or in me? This is by now old music.
We may do more asking for origins.

knowledge · doubt · identity
