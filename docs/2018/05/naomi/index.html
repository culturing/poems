Naomi

10 May 2018

https://poems.culturing.net/2018/05/naomi/

What a sweet thing,
when those eyes have the look of eyes
that are received.

I no longer find it hard to believe.

Between you and me are all secrets,
and we and the earth
are three.

A young man would swoon,
and an old disapprove,

But a man of both worlds gives soft praise.

love · women · beauty
