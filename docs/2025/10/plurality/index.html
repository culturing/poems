Plurality

12 October 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/10/plurality/

Why do I feel what I feel,
and what feeling would feel more like me
on this wine-dark sea, as traditions mingle
confounding the man who stays single
with truth: we are all braided lives,
made from strands of other lives,
and all feeling confirms this plurality.

identity · philosophy · memory
