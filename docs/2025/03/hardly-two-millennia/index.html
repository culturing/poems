Hardly two millennia

08 March 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/03/hardly-two-millennia/

What does it mean for Rome to fall,
to keep falling, as if through space,
a figment of the mind? It means
that we are free! The masters perish, and we
gleefully divide their very bones, and yet
we live on their estates or what is left of them.
