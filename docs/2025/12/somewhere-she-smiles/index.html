Somewhere She Smiles

26 December 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/12/somewhere-she-smiles/

Somewhere she smiles, and that light
bounces off the moon, and reaches me here.
It's unclear, but I do not care. My heart
is ahead of the curve and aware of what comes.
She does. She surely does.

longing · love · hope · women
