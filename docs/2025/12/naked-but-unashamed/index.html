Naked But Unashamed

12 December 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/12/naked-but-unashamed/

I should have known that you were looking,
should have felt those eyes dig into the meaning
of words such as these. And now I do.
I see you too, and under your gaze
I feel naked, but unashamed.

love · desire · identity
