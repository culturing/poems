Can't Be Put Out

16 December 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/12/cant-be-put-out/

We must not hide. I can hear you
cowering, see you squirming, feel you
desiring. But is it enough? You have fire
within you, chérie, and it can't be put out.

desire · women · love
