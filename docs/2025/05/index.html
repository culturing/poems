May 2025

30 May 2025

Labor PainsThese labor pains are for you,
On the Side of the EarthI shall plant myself firmly
Had You Been ThereHad you been there, you would know
Corners of StrangenessI am having the strangest life,
Many SmilesThere's a sort of haunting indiscretion,
How Much TimeHow much time must pass,
NothingNothing is what it claims to be.
You Carry It AlwaysThe Spectator steps through the door
Somewhere, SomehowWhat are we building? A kingdom,
Thinking BeginsThis is an impression made concrete,
Yeats' FootstepsIf I follow Yeats' footsteps
The Curtain FallsThe curtain falls on a people in pain.
All RiseIt is time to face your adversary,
