Just For Us

27 November 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/11/just-for-us/

I do not know what to say
or how to say it, what could soothe
the bruise or span the rift between...
but I am here, in the space
we've started making
just for us. Are you?

longing · love · home · hope
