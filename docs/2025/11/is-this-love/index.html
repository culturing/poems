Is This Love?

27 November 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/11/is-this-love/

Is this love? It might be,
though I strangely inquire
to sort out the truth and inspire
one last protest, before things are settled.
It's peaceful, and that is a welcome change.
A life where love lives is in range.

love · longing · hope
