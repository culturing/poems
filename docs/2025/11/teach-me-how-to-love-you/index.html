Teach Me How To Love You

27 November 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/11/teach-me-how-to-love-you/

Teach me how to love you,
and I will study well, for I must learn
not what love is, but what you are.
From afar I can learn only little.
I must come closer to see.
