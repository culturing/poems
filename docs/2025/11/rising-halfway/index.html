Rising Halfway

27 November 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/11/rising-halfway/

The sun is acting strange, it's in
a strange configuration, half exposed
above the horizon, yet half covered
by the earth. Who knows
if it rises or falls? Perhaps this time
it reverses its course and sets
after rising halfway.
