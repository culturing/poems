Through her I see

16 November 2025

https://poems.culturing.net/2025/11/through-her-i-see/

Through her I see
the undulations of chthonic energy,
vibrations of time and body
pulsing as one. It is her mysteries
that allow for life, its origin
and purpose, and she dares not fly
into the empty sky, where all is lost.
