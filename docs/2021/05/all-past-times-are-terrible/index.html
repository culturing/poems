All past times are terrible

28 May 2021

https://poems.culturing.net/2021/05/all-past-times-are-terrible/

All past times are terrible
to those who look back now,

Just like future times are hazy
to those gazing forward now.

Only now is real,
and now alone deserves the seal
of approbation,

For each solid nation
breaks when stretched too far.
