The Loss of Justice

26 October 2024

https://poems.culturing.net/2024/10/the-loss-of-justice/

These are the pieces of our lives
tossed over the board by a careless hand
which relinquishes claims to authority
and weakens its own grip on power.

The loss of Justice is the eternal theme,
for she remains queen of our hearts
though not queen of our hands.
