If I Yearn For MoreThe president stumbles, like so many times,
No MoreSomething in the soul cries out, "No more!"
A Time is ComingA time is coming (it always comes)
True HealingI have often thought over how
New HealingIs there another way, out past ways that were tried
Through the UnknownIf I venture out through the unknown,
More AuthenticSomething changed a hundred years ago,
CarefullyIf I wander out into the ether,
The FluxIf you really want to know what you are doing,
Response to a QueryYes, Athens is in the Bible,
