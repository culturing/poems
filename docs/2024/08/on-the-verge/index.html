On the Verge

11 August 2024

https://poems.culturing.net/2024/08/on-the-verge/

Here we go, on the verge
of ourselves, at the edge
of another tomorrow, awaiting
enlightenment, which comes from...where?
If ourselves, then for what are we waiting?
If not, then we've got it all wrong.

identity · doubt · knowledge
