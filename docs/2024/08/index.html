11 August 2024

Ancient MastersThe ancient masters still know best,
Everyone's WrongEveryone's wrong about everything,
On the VergeHere we go, on the verge
The ClockThe Clock is a cruel master, compelling
Where You Are SteppingPower attracts the power-hungry, who,
She's ThereI cannot find her, Sophia, the maiden
A WarningLost sheep want the shepherd's crook.
Good Left UndoneHow many wise men live quiet lives
Somewhere Other Than BeliefI see you there, pacing, remembering
Kindle the FlameKindle the flame, turn the hearthstones,
The Fulcrum of TimeIn the face of death, who stands?
