27 July 2024

AssassinationIn those moments when everything changes,
A StartLet this be a start, and let it
The Wind and My Place in ItCan anyone remember the point of it all,
JusticeHow does one answer them, naked
Things Unseen Though KnownIn the beginning, one finds God
Surely We Know BestIt is a miracle each time, but,
As All Time Passes ByLove is a reason to suffer,
Tethered to FreedomHow do I cope with it, spent
