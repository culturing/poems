America or Aristotle

13 January 2019

https://poems.culturing.net/2019/01/america-or-aristotle/

O land of confusion, burning bright!

Land with no middle term,
where “why?” is forbidden
because unanswerable,

Flee from thyself.
Find God.

He’s out there somewhere.
