Crickets For the Innocent

White Shade

07 July 2019

https://poems.culturing.net/2019/07/white-shade/

A shade of white,
not quite opaque,
disturbs my sight.

It has no form,
but haunts the night
like one unsteadily born.

The ashes of a pyre
lay where
She was burned bright.

I do not see the Lady,
and her absence
haunts my sight.

anima · grief
