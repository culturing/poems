Marriage

30 April 2019

https://poems.culturing.net/2019/04/marriage/

Ah, the fall,
‘twas most to be expected.
Not the landing.

Lost within some woman’s world,
maneuvering and bent,

the forces pull,
and they will not relent.

This life will be well spent.
