Lady in the Dark

03 August 2019

https://poems.culturing.net/2019/08/lady-in-the-dark/

Beneath the moon
I saw her too,

alone,
where null is true.

I did not dare come near,
but felt that here, of all
damned places,
least deserves her.

anima · solitude · beauty
