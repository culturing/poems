Moldy Thinking

28 July 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/07/moldy-thinking/

I can smell disaster ahead,
but nobody will listen. It's like
moldy thinking, the kind that has
sat in the sun for too long, gazing
stupidly skyward, festering,
waiting to be discarded.
