Come Again

20 March 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/03/come-again/

It has come again, this time
so new, having punctured the sky
I am writing to you, asking why,
and do not even try to deny it,
no, this is here now, here and now,
and tomorrow comes true
if once listened through you.

hope · time · faith
