2023

March 2023

20 March 2023

Plain and TrueI watch them working slowly,
Saving DaylightWhen is there an end to it, the wailing
Not even sure who to askEven the clock on my HVAC
As She WillShe grasped my hand, and off we flew
Larger Accomplishments (Pragmatism)Must they be true to be useful, or is it
So Very GreekIt is sad, don't you know, to be living here now
The Realm of ReasonHave we tired yet of clocks and steamships?
UnfriendlyAfter all these years one would have thought
Small All the SameCaught up withal in the most divine madness
The Drums of AlexanderAlexander stood finally tall on Persepolis
Come AgainIt has come again, this time
The Throne of CyrusI feel the weight of Cyrus,
Ahura Mazda (the Question Remains)All hail Ahura Mazda, all hail!
