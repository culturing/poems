16 June 2023

Blank SpaceIn a field of blank space
What Surprises RemainWith a small bit of horsepower,
Better JudgmentIt is time to reflect, to remember
DreamsWhen I consider the teenagers over the way,
No Single ForceI think that we know
Flying LessonsSoaring bird large
Ideal RepublicAsia has had no ideal republic,
Both How and WhyIt is unwise to blame bad sheep
Saying New SayingsThe Supreme Intelligence in all of its forms
Pain That One Calls HomeWe are out here searching for something,
Uncivilized After All These YearsBy the power invested in the personless rationality
HabitsWhere is it going, this spiralling void
Corporate ManIt's a god! It's a hero!
Rolling WavesI am going out to sea,
Some MenSome men are born
EudaimoniaIn a room of thinkers, I was quiet,
Banishing NightNo great poet has ever made a difference,
