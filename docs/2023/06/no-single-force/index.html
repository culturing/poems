No Single Force

16 June 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/06/no-single-force/

I think that we know
very little, that what we predict
may come true, or may not,
that no laws constrain nature,
that nature is plural,
that no single force drives it all.

nature · doubt · knowledge
