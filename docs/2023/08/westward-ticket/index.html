Westward Ticket

17 August 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/08/westward-ticket/

I have forgone my westward ticket
and set up shop here. It is lonely
for now, but that's not the worst thing.
It's not worse than bad company.
Sometimes the owls hoot at dusk,
but I never have seen them fly.
With a little more coaxing, perhaps.
