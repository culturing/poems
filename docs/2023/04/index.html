April 2023

26 April 2023

Wait, Think, SpeakStillborn I fell through the silence of water
Where They Can See YouYour feelings may threaten their power,
They May Be RightI was here before, but did not know it.
The Thin VeilHow many graves will it take before
Menagerie of RulesThere are no universalizable maxims,
MonotheismWill monotheism suffice?
Stop the BombingAmazing isn't it, how easily one now
The ScientistThe scientist pauses, haunched
ContritionI believe we are waiting for Caesar,
The ProtestantThe Protestant weeps
Some OtherUpon the way backward
DutyThere are those who accept the old Duty
WallowWho can stand them, obsessed
Untold MiseryHow can there have been so much
Good EyesI was born sighted in a tribe of the blind,
Little BirdLittle bird sings of the break of spring,
The Way of the NightLike no one's business, I entered
culminationYou would think that by now
Who are we?The peoples of the Mediterranean
Something About PlatoThere's just something about Plato
The Question of DemocracyThe People are not worthy of their servants
SourcesNo more the uneducated masses,
On Whose AuthorityThe people demand a show, like always,
What time is thisThey are proud to have learned their lessons
Some NaturesSome natures are harmful,
If He Would Speak TodayIf he would speak today, the Poet
At EleusisFollow these steps, if you dare
