What time is this

26 April 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/04/what-time-is-this/

They are proud to have learned their lessons
and never question. I wince in their presence.
I have never learned their lessons. What time is this,
that comes when all are sleeping, like a cock about to crow?
