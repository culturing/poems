Untold Misery

26 April 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/04/untold-misery/

How can there have been so much
and be so little remaining? Time
with its cup of lethe, panacea,
so they say. But what good is it,
being lost, without even wounds for guidance?

time · memory · grief
