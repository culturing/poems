Who are we?

26 April 2023

https://poems.culturing.net/2023/04/who-are-we/

The peoples of the Mediterranean
were always pretending to be gods
or descended from gods or becoming
like gods, and one wonders how far
this can take us. If creatures
like us act like gods, it would
surely destroy us, but if we do not,
then who are we?
