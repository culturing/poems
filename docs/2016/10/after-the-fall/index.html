After the Fall

28 October 2016

https://poems.culturing.net/2016/10/after-the-fall/

I dislike Rome
    (he whispered
    to no one in particular),

Her dead-head poets,
her worldliness,

But then I am a Platonist,
who finds no
succor colliding
επιθυμια with time.

What Good are monuments?
All of them fall.

No, no, no Godot,
no Reason,

But I make with you,

and sing a nascent treason.

    Is that true?
