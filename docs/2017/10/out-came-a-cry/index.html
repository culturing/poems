Out came a cry

16 October 2017

https://poems.culturing.net/2017/10/out-came-a-cry/

Out came a cry
    from beneath the great Nothing,
but no one was there to believe it.

An oomph went woomph,
    and the meaning went missing,
and no one was there to retrieve it.

And day was like sand,
    and the moon went away,
and nobody was there to be free with.

doubt · solitude · faith
