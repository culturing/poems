Always

07 July 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/07/07/always/

I don't know why my eyes
chose this time to awaken,
or why you were there to fulfill my gaze,
but I know that my life has been changed,
and some part of me will love some part of you
always.

love · time · memory
