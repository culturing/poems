Jesus Again

07 July 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/07/07/jesus-again/

I remember a Jesus
who meant the world and more to me,
who led my way.

He always knew just what to say,
and I needed his words to inform
my formlessness.

I feel warmly for him,
though my pantheon grows,
for the seeds he sowed
have grown ripe for harvest.

faith · memory · myth
