Show Me the Goddess

07 July 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/07/07/show-me-the-goddess/

Show me the goddess in you
so that I may love her
and grow into a better man,

but do not expect me to kneel forever.
Your temple is not my destination.

anima · love · women
