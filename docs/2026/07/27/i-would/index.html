I Would

27 July 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/07/27/i-would/

Would that you would hear my call,
would that love would come first, then fall
down to earth, would that you'd know your worth
and I mine, would that we would transpire
together, grow tall, would that love would
be easy, would that songs would fill up the hall.
I would. I would.

longing · love · hope · desire
