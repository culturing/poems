Inexperienced

16 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/inexperienced/

Maybe I am the inexperienced one.
You are woman, and I will allow you
to show me what that means.

And yet I can dream, can't I,
about what "woman" means? I would be
a poor poet if I did not have such dreams.

An artist must sculpt with precision.
A lover lets blemishes be.
