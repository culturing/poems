I Will Live Well

05 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/i-will-live-well/

Perhaps this was my story after all, and all along
these changes meant my progress, no one else's. Who's to say?
At the end of the day, I own my life only, if that,
and I will live well, as well as the stories tell.

identity · freedom · hope
