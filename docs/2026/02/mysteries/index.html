Mysteries

05 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/mysteries/

I know better than to expect that a woman
can be understood, but I must try anyway,
for my own benefit and edification. She is no different
than all other mysteries...and no less deep.

women · knowledge · doubt
