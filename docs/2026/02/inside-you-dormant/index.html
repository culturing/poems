Inside You, Dormant

21 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/inside-you-dormant/

The strength you seek
is inside you, dormant,
waiting for some alarm
to wake it up. This is why
you crave danger, and this
is why only you can answer
the questions that life is asking you.

I am the alarm.
How will you answer?
