Succubus

21 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/succubus/

So, you are a succubus, is that it? You're hardly the first.
But if so, you're not welcome here. Go back where you belong.

But no, you don't belong with the succubi either.
There's too much good in you.
