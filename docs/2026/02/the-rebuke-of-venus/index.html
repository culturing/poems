The Rebuke of Venus

27 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/the-rebuke-of-venus/

Which of us deserved the rebuke of Venus?
I insist that my love was good.
But I will not insist where that love is not valued,
or where I am not respected.

myth · love · power
