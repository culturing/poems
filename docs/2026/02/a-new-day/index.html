A New Day

27 February 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/02/a-new-day/

I am glad that you have awoken.
It could never have been painless.
Somehow I can feel a change in the air.
There was something strange about the morning
of the day that I saw you. Was it perhaps
a new day in more ways than one?
