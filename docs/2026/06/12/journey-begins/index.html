Journey Begins

12 June 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/06/12/journey-begins/

Your journey begins, and I
am alone with myself again,
but in peace, with a new half
awoken and ready to greet those
whose path I cross. I meet obstacles,
ready to honestly face them.

healing · solitude · hope
