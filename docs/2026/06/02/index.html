Sacred FireI live my life
Not the SkyI called to you across the stars.
The Purpose of PowerThe purpose of power is health,
CodependencyHave I uncovered my own codependency,
In Open AirThrough that door, into mystical grace,
The Dark LadyThe Dark Lady swirls around and around
