If I Could

07 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/07/if-i-could/

If I could soothe your pain
with words of love, I would speak
until out of breath. If I could sing
to make you smile, if I could hold you
for a while, if I could answer
all your questions, I would know
that I lived well.
