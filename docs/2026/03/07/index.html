07 March 2026

Raphael's MadonnaI paint her in colors worthy
First DanceWhen you're ready,
Full GravityBut love is a complex maneuver, requiring
If I CouldIf I could soothe your pain
Gaseous At LastI will seek answers
For So Long I've DreamedTime passes, day turns into day,
Strange PredicamentWhat a strange predicament.
