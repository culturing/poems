L'amour n'est pas

16 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/16/lamour-nest-pas/

Thank you for trusting me.
I hope I have led you well
through the dark woods of passion.
I hope you can find your way now.
I will never forget you.
L'amour n'est pas un long fleuve tranquille.
But it is the rapids that allow us to heal.

healing · love · grief · hope
