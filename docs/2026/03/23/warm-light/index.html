Warm Light

23 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/23/warm-light/

The sun burns, shines its light between spaces
long left in the dark, and heats up
the cold stones of the heart. I will warm
in its light, feel its heat, let it liven
the sleepy parts of me. And then I will be free.

healing · hope · nature · freedom
