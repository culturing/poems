Grains of Salt

15 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/15/grains-of-salt/

Feel free to ignore my song, as it passes through
these dissonant bars. It will sort itself out.
This pain must be digested here, but I do not claim
infallibility. This is not the place to build trust
or to speak only positive words. This is where
the dark words can take shape and do battle
against truth. But truth always wins in the long run.
