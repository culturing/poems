For What Purpose

31 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/31/for-what-purpose/

Once again wishing to burn the whole thing down,
life and its accoutrements, vanity after vanity
until final rest. For what purpose? Just to prolong
the struggle? Some days I just cannot find reasons.
