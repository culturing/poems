Whoever You Are

31 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/31/whoever-you-are/

If some other tomorrow follows
after awakening, if there are flowers
growing in fallow fields, if spring does come,
snow melts, and the sun shines out anew,
I will respond to you, the one who comes,
whoever you are.

hope · love · nature
