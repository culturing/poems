The Mad Goddess II

31 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/31/the-mad-goddess-ii/

The mad goddess no longer speaks,
though she sits in the corner, brooding
with daggers in her eyes.

She will tolerate no further transgressions.

She has joined the pantheon, taking her place
in the seat of Dionysus.
