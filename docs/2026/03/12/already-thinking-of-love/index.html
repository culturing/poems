Already Thinking of Love

12 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/12/already-thinking-of-love/

How did I know already last March
to expect renovation? Was I already thinking of love?
Were you already thinking of me? Or did Love
have us both in mind?

love · time · memory
