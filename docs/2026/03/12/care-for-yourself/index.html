Care For Yourself

12 March 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/03/12/care-for-yourself/

You must care for yourself as a primary injunction,
because otherwise your needs become someone else's problem.
It's prosocial to care for yourself, to take care of yourself,
because otherwise someone else has to.
