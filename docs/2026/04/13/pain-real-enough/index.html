Pain, Real Enough

13 April 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/04/13/pain-real-enough/

I have known pain, real enough
to call into question the sanity of living,
to shake the foundations of mind and body,
to rupture the vital energies. This pain
brings with it a clarity that few understand...
some things really matter. Most do not.

healing · grief · knowledge · mortality
