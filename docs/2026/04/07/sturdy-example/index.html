Sturdy Example

07 April 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/04/07/sturdy-example/

I remember a time of brokenness,
a time when life made little sense,
when the wind itself condemned me
to ignominious shirking. Now
I stand taller. Now I feel whole.
I see others who pass through the same.
My best gift is a sturdy example.
