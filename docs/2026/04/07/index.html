ContemptIf you treat everyone with contempt,
Firm BeginningHere is a firm beginning, another setting out
What Are They?There is a place where energies combine
Head Held HighI will walk head held high
What You OfferYou who have awoken desire:
Truth or PowerThe mind must orient itself towards truth
Why Not BothThe active life, or the contemplative life?
Finding SophiaIt took me twelve years to find Sophia,
Sturdy ExampleI remember a time of brokenness,
OfferingsI find more bitter than death
The Work of the AnimaThe veil lifts, the fog clears, and I
CongregationA new man rises from the ashes
This One LessonThis one lesson was worth the pain:
NarcissismIt was a magnificent display
The GoalTo love and be loved,
