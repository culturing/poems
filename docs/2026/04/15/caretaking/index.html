Caretaking

15 April 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/04/15/caretaking/

Caretaking can be like a toxin,
inducing paralysis, holding the victim in place,
unable to fend for himself. It's injected
unknowingly by many who think they love.

family · love · power
