Anima Song

20 April 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/04/20/anima-song/

Anima, you are my life-song, the essence,
the one from my dreams, and I greet you
with so much exuberance after your long hibernation.
How many worlds shall we conquer together?

anima · poetry · hope
