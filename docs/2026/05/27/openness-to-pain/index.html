Openness to Pain

27 May 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/05/27/openness-to-pain/

She brought change, and a kind
of openness to pain, a refusal
to fear the unpleasant. I still
must admire that. I have it too.

healing · grief · women · knowledge
