This Holy Place

21 May 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/05/21/this-holy-place/

I see again those hidden mysteries,
ancient unwavering stones at the basis
of all that we are. They cannot be toppled
or shaken by storms. We are where we are
despite protestations, despite deceptive chagrin.
I invite you to see this holy place,
a place open to those with pure hearts.
