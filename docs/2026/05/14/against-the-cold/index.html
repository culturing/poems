Against the Cold

14 May 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/05/14/against-the-cold/

Now I see across the horizon
approaching caravans carrying answers
to many unasked questions. I
am asking now. Pray tell
what must be told, as I
make bold to love myself
against the cold.

strength · hope · solitude
