Shadow Reflected

22 November 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/11/22/shadow-reflected/

She sees her shadow reflected
in others, she dances under the moon.
She lives in the shade of twilight.
She loves too soon. In her room the sun
dwindles, it leaves no sign of day,
as her moments gather steam,
then flutter away.

identity · women · love
