Shattered Sky

24 November 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/11/24/shattered-sky/

When will these tethers relent,
the invisible ties that bind my heart to yours
across space and time...I can only rhyme,
I can only decide to obey what climbs
from my depths toward sky...will you answer,
whoever you are?

Somebody knows, somebody has to know,
I just cannot believe such things go on unknown,
across chasms of years in the shadows of time,
you are mine, I am yours, we combine.

The sky shattered by the new moon
points sharply down into places too little explored...
the sun hides like it always does. Underneath
we express ourselves plainly in haunting refrains.
Who can answer us? Who knows the way?

It will be a new day, it will be
a new start, and my heart will go on
being heartful, there's no other way.

But the answers continue to flee
and elude the most careful investigations.
Who knows the truth? Why know? Why not feel?

I turn my soft heart into steel
and await disembarkment on shores
that will let me feel settled,
will let me feel home.

longing · anima · knowledge · home
