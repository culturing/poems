Can't Be Swayed

15 November 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/11/15/cant-be-swayed/

Can't be swayed, I can't be swayed,
the crickets chirp at love delayed,
and even though some love was made,
the ransom was not paid.

love · strength · longing
