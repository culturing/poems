Through This Experience

22 January 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/01/through-this-experience/

I think I can love you. That's a start.
Maybe I'll teach you some things.
Surely you'll teach me some things.
And maybe we'll learn some together.

love · knowledge · hope
