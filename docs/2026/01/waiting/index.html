Waiting

31 January 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/01/waiting/

We are waiting to feel ready. We are waiting
until the right time. We are waiting until
it feels right. We are waiting. But may I ask
whether these things might be made true
by spending time together?

longing · love · time
