Tell Me About Yourself

08 January 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/01/tell-me-about-yourself/

Now that we've fallen in love,
I suppose it's as good a time as any
to get to know each other. Wouldn't you agree?
Seems a bit out of order, but hey
I'm here for it. Tell me about yourself.

love · knowledge · desire
