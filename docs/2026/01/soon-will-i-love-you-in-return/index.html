Soon Will I Love You In Return

16 January 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/01/soon-will-i-love-you-in-return/

If you use love (or love's withdrawal) as a weapon, a tool for manipulation,
then soon it will become poisonous, soon I will not even want it,
soon I will leave you to your poisoned love. But if you love
as a gift, given freely...then soon will I love you in return.

love · power · freedom
