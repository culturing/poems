Adagio Romance

03 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/03/adagio-romance/

She wonders. He waits.
The call comes from above
and underneath. It would be
an adagio romance, in the key
of seeing and being seen. Is it magic?
Or are they deceived?
