Another Me

03 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/03/another-me/

I arrive again at the strangest place,
the strange vista wherefrom I detect in the distance
another me, coming with certainty, ready to live
here, now. But who is he really? And why
is she here, still carving out space for herself?

identity · love · doubt
