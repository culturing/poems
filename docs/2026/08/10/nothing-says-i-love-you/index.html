Nothing Says I Love You

10 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/10/nothing-says-i-love-you/

Nothing says "I love you"
like an angry letter, sent
in despair. It proves the feeling,
even as it dies. It shows
that one was not joking. It burns
what one cannot have.
