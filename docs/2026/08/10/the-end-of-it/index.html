The End of It

10 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/10/the-end-of-it/

This is the end of it, this
is the point of the journey where love
becomes possible. This is the last hope
made manifest, the final answer
to the question one had been afraid to ask.

longing · love · hope · identity
