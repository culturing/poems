Think

10 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/10/think/

There's a madness to my method, but I think
I know what I'm doing. Yes, I think
I can find my way. You know
I have come to remind you
that time keeps on flowing. You know
that it comes and it goes on its own. I think
there are answers that bubble up in what we think.
