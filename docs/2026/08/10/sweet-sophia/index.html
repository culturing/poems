Sweet Sophia

10 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/10/sweet-sophia/

How many songs shall I write for you!
Muse of the stars, knowing all that we are,
sweet Sophia or manifest grace, you are flowing
above, over, through all things. You are here,
I have found you, I know your name
and your presence. I wait for your sweet embrace.
