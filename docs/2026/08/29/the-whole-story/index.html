The Whole Story

29 August 2026

https://poems.culturing.net/2026/08/29/the-whole-story/

My mother didn't hug me enough
(or did she hug me too much?)
and now I must face
the consequences. Still she appears
a fine woman, to those unaffected,
and even to me on my better days.
Which is the truth? Can any truth
really contain the whole story?

family · doubt · knowledge
