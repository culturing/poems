**To the Girl on the Bus Tour in Banff
28 September 2026

I never learned your name,
but I knew your face long before
this strange gravity brought us around
and around again to each other.
You were not yet wise enough to survive
the encounter, and so I refrained,
but I saw you wonder as I too wondered
how such things come to pass and whether
their meaning is true or the illusion
of two lonely souls seeking home.
