**To the Girl in the Library
15 October 2026

You catch my eye and I yours.
We share a forbidden moment 
of electric possibility. You hide,
you return to the scene of the glance,
you slide and provide
a better view, you pass by
on your way to the books behind,
and I notice your scent.
Do you know what you're doing?
Or is this how you learn? I see 
you need something from the bottom shelf,
but remember this lesson: I like your dance, 
but save it 
until you can love and be loved in return.
