There is a class of statements
that we only say when they are false,
*because* they are false. They are soothing
illusions, designed to counterbalance
uncomfortable truths. Beware the soothing
of silver-tongued manipulators! Don't
let their false be your true. But
we need some illusions to survive here.
We simply can't swallow all truths.
