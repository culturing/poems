**Madness of Stars
29 August 2026

Sophia, your madness shimmers now
out of the ether, in song made lovely
by chaos brought into form. I can hear you
even now, and I don't know how, but you answer
my call with your own, in unison, always
reminding, remembering, ready to speak
and inform what needs forming. So come
fill my life with your song, as I answer
with love and the madness of stars. This
is all that we are or ever shall be.
