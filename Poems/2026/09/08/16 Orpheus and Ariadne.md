**Orpheus and Ariadne
08 September 2026

Orpheus emerges on Naxos
and finds Ariadne, half-nude,
awaiting her lover, enjoying the sun.
Both have been abandoned, but 
recognize in each other a sacred power,
the strength to lead others through danger.
What music awaits them?
Can sacral harmonies disrupt
the fatal ending, and lead both into
a new story?
