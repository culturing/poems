**Starlady
24 October 2026

I come again to this sacred space,
naked and ready to learn...who
can teach me? Who knows the way
down from heaven and into ourselves,
when those very selves carry heaven,
and earth waits with gravity,
taunting any Icarus? Who knows
if not the inner guide, sweetest muse,
the starlady who speaks in riddles
and answers all answers with questions?
