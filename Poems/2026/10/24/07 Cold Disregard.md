**Cold Disregard
24 October 2026

Why this sudden anger, bouncing
from pain to pain, as if
to assuage some deep grief hidden
down in some vein...I can claim
to be happy, I can claim to be free,
but between you and me there are chasms
that blank and deceive, and beyond them
I only see Nothing, the dark shade
of life's unperturbable silence, the cold
disregard into which all desire must fade.
