> A woman waits for me, she contains all, nothing is lacking,
> Yet all were lacking if sex were lacking, or if the moisture of the right man were lacking.
-- Whitman, *A Woman Waits For Me*

You have a spiritualized sexuality. You, though a master of spirit,
are unacquainted with the wilderness of bodies, including (perhaps) your own body.
You must get acquainted. I do not know how, but you know. Trust yourself.
Let your body explain.
