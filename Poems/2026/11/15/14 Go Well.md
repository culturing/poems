**Go Well
15 November 2026

I will not forget you, Antheia,
I will not forget how your flowers
remembered what blooming means, or how love
felt so close to coming true. I see you,
and I always will see...as I know you saw me,
through the veil of the too-opaque shapes
to the heart of the matter. I'll miss you,
I'll always miss you, and yet we both know
that we have other places to go. So go well.
I will leave here your story, and tell
of my own, though it cannot be told without you.
