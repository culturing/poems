*Fallow Hearts II
22 November 2026

She wanders through darkness, aghast
at the shapeshifting forms of man lost
man found man lost again. There was no other way,
but her voices declare all lost. They say
to go on alone, to deny any hope
as a form of protection. Does there come a day
when protection suffices, when love can bloom and spread roots...
or are some hearts too fallow?
