*Antigravity
22 November 2026

Like the noble gases, he refuses to bond,
floating always somewhere between nuclear forces,
unable to settle down. What gravity
dares to impose on such elegant refusal?
Or was it not elegant at all?

---

He wished for attachment but could do no more
than wish, being made out of antigravity
by some deep affliction too lately understood.
But the vestigial call, the phantom itch of desire
for another would torment his diminished capacity
to the end of his days.

---

Somewhere was there a black hole
with enough drawing power to pull even him
into some kind of orbit? And what
would he find there but darkness?
