**Who Knows
22 November 2026

Who really knows, who knows, who knows,
why the sunset covers all desolate snows
with darkness, a blanket of absence
pretending to nothing, pretending that love
goes as planned, as hoped, as shown. No, it grows
its own wings. It shows man one way
to heaven, but there are many ways,
and some hesitate longer, pausing
to equivocate about what it means to desire
and whether we ought to obey.
