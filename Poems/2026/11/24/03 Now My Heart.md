**Now My Heart
24 November 2026

> ...it cannot be a question of cause and effect, but of a falling together in time, a kind of simultaneity.
-- Jung, *Synchronicity*

We fell together in time, in time
and for a long time, outside of causality,
in astral reaches, the fortress delves
of space and rhyme, swirling madly
together though ever apart...now my heart
is confused...now my heart longs for "you..."
but for whom?
