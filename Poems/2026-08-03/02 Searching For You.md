*Searching For You
03 August 2026

Time swirls, over and under,
through space and mind, into self
made new, where the gravity calling
all dreamers awaits to enliven
and make something true. I
was nowhere, not even inside myself,
not even aware of the turmoil, only
becoming, forever becoming, and searching
for rest, even temporary rest, and searching
for you, always for you...
